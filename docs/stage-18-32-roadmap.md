# The Vault — Restored Long Roadmap

Stage 17 is the Reconstruction Alpha checkpoint, not the end of the full project.

## Stage 18 — Custom Collection Editor

Create personal collections, edit descriptions and notes, manage ordered membership, archive collections without deletion, and undo changes.

## Stage 19 — Expedition Builder

Create cross-wing quests with required, optional, and hidden objectives; preserve completion and abandonment history.

## Stage 20 — Achievement Workshop

Expand public and classified achievements, evidence records, rarity, secret chains, and deliberately strange unlocks.

## Stage 21 — Unlockable Progression

Use meaningful XP for titles, cosmetics, command skins, sound settings, hidden rooms, and decorations without locking core functions.

## Stage 22 — Advanced Command Language

Support composable filters, dates, runtimes, ratings, ownership, progress, relationships, saved queries, and explainable results.

## Stage 23 — Deep Spoiler Rules

Apply progress-sensitive clearance to episode names, artwork, descriptions, characters, trivia, and classified achievements.

## Stage 24 — Layout-Changing Themes

Make Video Store, Restricted Archive, Windows 95, SNES RPG, and Bunker Terminal swap component metaphors and layouts.

## Stage 25 — Personal Eras

Detect evidence-backed periods of interest from canonical history without inventing dates for imported records.

Status: complete at the Stage 27 temporal-history checkpoint.

## Stage 26 — Anniversaries and Taste Evolution

Add On This Day, first-versus-later ratings, revisits, yearly comparisons, and grounded taste-change reports.

Status: complete at the Stage 27 temporal-history checkpoint.

## Stage 27 — Seasonal and Rare Vault Moments

Add restrained seasonal behavior, archival echoes, collection ceremonies, and rare time-based secrets.

Status: complete. Calendar moments deduplicate annually, collection ceremonies deduplicate per sealed collection, and streaks remain disabled.

## Stage 28 — Real Import Pipelines

Build validated, reversible intake for YouTube, music, podcasts, books/manga, food, trips, and calendar data as real exports become available.

Status: complete at the Stage 29 import-and-relationship checkpoint. Local JSON, CSV, TSV, and ICS intake is previewed, protected, deduplicated, evidence-preserving, and rollback-capable.

## Stage 29 — Cross-Vault Relationship Graph

Connect people, franchises, genres, places, adaptations, collections, and experiences with stable relationship records.

Status: complete. Derived genre and collection links stay live; imported and manual links are stable evidence records with protected change paths.

## Stage 30 — Grounded Vault Oracle

Add natural-language querying over structured Vault truth with citations back to records and canonical events.

Status: complete at the Stage 31 supervised-intelligence checkpoint. The Oracle is local, deterministic, citation-carrying, and refuses unsupported questions instead of guessing.

## Stage 31 — Supervised Automation

Extend self-maintenance with confidence policies, dry runs, approval gates, rollback, and failure isolation.

Status: complete. All execution requires a reviewed dry run and explicit approval; metadata mutation is snapshotted, journaled, conflict-aware, and blocked after earlier failure or attention.

## Stage 32 — Complete-System Audit

Run migration, corruption, restore, performance, accessibility, recovery, and live-library drills; document known limits and create the final recovery seal.

Status: complete. The eight-part audit passed after fixing one unnamed hidden control and mobile overflow. Schema 32 is the final seal for the original reconstruction roadmap.
