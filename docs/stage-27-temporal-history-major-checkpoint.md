# Stage 27 — Temporal History Major Checkpoint

Completed July 30, 2026.

## Scope

This checkpoint completes Stages 25–27 of the restored long roadmap:

- Personal Eras derived from meaningful canonical events, grouped by quarter only when at least three events support the period
- Archival Echoes for truthful On This Day anniversaries, yearly rating comparisons, and witnessed revisits
- rare calendar moments with annual deduplication
- one-time collection-sealing ceremonies backed by the canonical seal event
- no attendance streaks, retroactive dates, or inferred imported history

## Safety and truth rules

- Passive Vault-open, wing-visit, and library-scan noise cannot create eras or taste reports.
- Era evidence retains the exact supporting event IDs and chronological bounds.
- Rating evolution uses only rating events containing a recorded numeric destination value.
- Seasonal moments retain their trigger rule, local date, timestamp, and unique period key.
- Collection ceremonies retain the collection ID, seal event ID, and sealed record count.
- Health validation rejects incomplete policy metadata, malformed moments, duplicate moment IDs, duplicate trigger keys, invalid timestamps, or missing evidence.
